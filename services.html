Lifebelt Poetry

Independent poetry publishing with a cohesive house style, where you keep 100% of your rights.

Lifebelt Press offers assisted publishing for poets seeking a professionally produced pamphlet or collection (see A Necessary Presence as our house-style model). You retain full copyright and all proceeds from your direct sales.

3. How It Works & Payment Schedule

What you supply: A fully edited and copy-edited manuscript (.doc/.docx/.rtf), short cover blurb, longer description and any advance puffs, short contributor bio(s), optional 300dpi cover/interior artwork, and your own payment links for direct sales.
Invoicing: 50% on manuscript handover; 50% (plus hard costs and any extra hourly work at £40/hr) on approval of print PDFs. Full Distribution Add-on fees are invoiced 100% upon approval of digital POD proofs.
